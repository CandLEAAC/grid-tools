    Questions

    What does this tool change?

    Only the pictures stored inside the gridset (for example screenshots and photos). Words, symbols, buttons, sounds, links and settings are copied across exactly as they were. Widgit symbols are not touched.

    Will the pictures look worse?

    They should look the same. Every picture is checked against the original after it is made smaller, and if it would look different the original picture is kept instead. See-through parts of pictures stay see-through.

    Why was my gridset rejected?

    Encrypted (protected) gridsets, usually ending in .gridsetx, cannot be opened by this tool. Please use the original .gridset file instead.

    Is my gridset uploaded anywhere?

    No. All the work happens inside your web browser, on your computer. The gridset is never sent over the internet.
